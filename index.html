How it works
  • Beat the dealer's hand without going over 21. Face cards count 10; aces count 11 unless that busts you, then 1.
  • A two-card 21 (blackjack) pays 3:2. Dealer peeks and stands on all 17s.
  • Double doubles your bet, takes exactly one more card, then stands.
  • Ties push — your bet comes back.

How it works
  • Two hole cards each. Five community cards arrive as flop (3), turn (1), river (1). Best five-card hand wins the pot.
  • Blinds are 10 / 20 and rotate each hand. Raises are fixed steps of +50 or +150 over the current bet — a simplified no-limit.
  • Ava plays tight, Marcus plays loose and bluffs, June is steady. Their stacks refill to 1,000 when they go broke; yours is your shared wallet.
  • Simplification: no side pots — if a short stack is all-in, they contest the whole pot.

How it works
  • Play a card that matches the top card's color, number, or symbol — or any Wild. Glowing cards in your hand are playable.
  • Skip jumps the next player · Reverse flips direction · +2 makes the next player draw two and lose their turn · Wild +4 does the same with four.
  • No match? Tap the draw pile for one card. If it plays, you can use it right away; otherwise tap Pass.
  • First to play their last card wins the round.
